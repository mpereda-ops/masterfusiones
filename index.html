§ 01 · The problem

The most expensive decisions
are made without rehearsal.

Critical organizational decisions — restructuring a team, closing a negotiation, entering a new market, defusing a conflict — get made once, in real time, with consequences that compound for years. Most leaders face them armed with theory they once memorized and never practiced.

Pilots don't fly that way. Surgeons don't operate that way. Organizational decision-makers shouldn't either.

SimuLab-AI is a simulation environment where those decisions get rehearsed before they're made for real. AI-driven characters react. Behavioral metrics move. Consequences unfold. And the theoretical framework that explains each decision arrives in the moment, not before.

§ 02 · The platform

Four layers.
One rehearsal system.

Every SimuLab-AI program combines four layers. Separately, they already exist in different industries. Together, they form a coherent rehearsal environment for organizational decisions.

01
Foundation

Expert-led preparation

Each simulator is preceded by hours of preparation delivered by senior professionals from the relevant field — negotiators, mediators, foreign trade operators, innovation leads, executives in active practice. Duration adjusts to the program, from four to twenty hours.

02
Practice

Live simulation

The participant enters a calibrated scenario with characters, dynamic metrics and a running clock. AI-driven characters react in real time. Behavioral consequences become visible. The scenario evolves with every decision.

03
Feedback

Behavioral analysis

After each decision the system returns the underlying decision logic, the precedent literature, and the second-order consequences. Not content to study. A mirror of what just happened, in the moment.

04
Intelligence

Decision intelligence

Every decision is logged. Participants receive a personalized analytical report; the program owner receives an aggregated rubric across cohorts. Patterns of behavior, recurring blind spots, organizational dynamics become measurable.

§ 06 · The differential

Your organization.
Your parameters. Your simulator.

Any rehearsal environment in the catalog can be calibrated with real data from the organization that will use it. Workforce, sector, market, constraints, the moment the organization is going through. SimuLab-AI absorbs that data and builds the scenario your teams will decide on. Not a Harvard case. Yours.

What you upload

The organization, as it is.

Workforce with ages, areas, seniority and roles. Relevant financial or operational indicators. Live tensions described by the program owner. Sector and market characteristics. Organizational constraints — structure, incentive systems, declared and observed culture. Upload via form, CSV or briefing session.

How it translates

A scenario that recognizes you.

Simulator characters are rewritten to mirror your org chart. Initial metrics reflect the real state of the team, not an academic average. Dilemmas are built on the tensions your organization already has. Theoretical feedback adapts to the sector — banking is not healthcare is not public service.

What it's for

Decisions trained before they're made.

For boards to rehearse a hard decision before making it for real. For consultancies to validate hypotheses before intervening. For executive development to stop talking about "teams in general" and start talking about the specific team in front of the director.

Evidence-based design over operational reality.

§ 08 · Academic depth

Evidence-based design.
Optional certification by USAL.

Every rehearsal environment builds on a documented evidence base with visible, traceable references. Preparation is delivered by senior professionals and instructors with proven track records. AI acts as a facilitator — never as a substitute for the human in the loop.

When requested, programs can incorporate official certification by the University of Salamanca as continuing education courses or microcredentials, through the Centro de Formación Permanente.

AI doesn't replace the instructor. It returns time for what only a human can do.
§ 09 · Who's behind

University of Salamanca
faculty with senior
business experience.

SimuLab-AI is an academic initiative developed by a group of faculty from the University of Salamanca with consolidated track records in teaching and research. The project brings together specialists in negotiation, conflict management, knowledge management, innovation, business models, international trade and team development.

Alongside that academic core, the project incorporates professionals in active practice with senior business backgrounds — executives, consultants and operators with direct experience in banking, foreign trade, organizational transformation, corporate innovation and team leadership in large companies. Their role is central, not complementary.

The combination is what distinguishes SimuLab-AI. Evidence-based frameworks from a university with eight centuries of history, translated into operational reality by people who have made those decisions for real.

Academic core

Faculty of the Department of Business Administration of the University of Salamanca. Active teaching across undergraduate, master's and continuing education. Research published in indexed journals.

Business core

Executives and consultants in active practice. Decades of accumulated experience in banking, foreign trade, organizational transformation and team leadership across multinational and mid-cap firms.
